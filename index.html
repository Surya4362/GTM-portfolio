Case studies

What was broken, what I built, what moved.

Problem

  • One mixed-audience campaign
  • The same long, emoji and link-heavy message for about a year
  • No reply segmentation
  • Lead flow trending down

What I built

  • Persona campaigns for finance leadership, AP, procurement and IT/ERP, each with its own 4-message sequence
  • Reply buckets (Interested, Not Sure, No Requirement, Gave Contact) with follow-up timing per bucket
  • Segmented event follow-up: attended, not attended, interacted
  • Webinar invites with duplicate checks
  • Weekly reporting and a funnel tracker for management
  • Inactive sender accounts removed, volume rebalanced

Result

  • 200+ qualified leads in 4 months
  • Acceptance reached 28% in October
  • Replies turning into leads: ~10% to ~14%
  • Mid-September dip diagnosed and fixed within weeks
  • Event follow-up: ~11% reply rate, ~49% acceptance (early, small sample)

Problem

  • Reach CFOs, Finance Heads, AP Managers and Procurement Heads at mid to large Indian enterprises
  • With a product they had never heard of

What I built

  • A defined ICP across firmographics, titles and tech filters, used in Apollo and Sales Navigator
  • One multi-profile Apollo-data campaign: view profile, like post, connect, then messages with one link each (calculator, video, demo booking) and a breakup message
  • Cold email on 4 new mailboxes in Apollo, a deliverability test, and a 6-touch sequence that sends clickers to follow-ups and unsubscribers to suppression

Result

  • ~5% reply rate in the first weeks, against ~3% for the previous Sales Navigator campaign
  • Early read, still running

Experiments log

Tested X against Y. Kept what won.

Won

Message length

Long, emoji and link-heavy messages against one pain point under 200 characters with no promotional language. The short message won on response rate.

Early win

Event follow-up

Segmented by attended, not attended and interacted. The non-attendee sequence reached about 11% reply rate.

Running

One link per message

Calculator, video and demo booking link, each in its own message, to see which asset earns the most replies.

Running

Persona campaigns

One mixed list against campaigns split by buyer role. Early results are positive.

Running

Mailbox rebuild

Email moved to 4 new mailboxes after inbox placement on the old one was damaged. Warm-up and deliverability testing in progress.
